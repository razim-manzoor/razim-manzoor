# Portfolio inspiration study

Checked: 2026-10-09. Scope: expanded research before implementation. No application or dependency changes.

## Method and evidence

Start with Awwwards' [annual awards](https://www.awwwards.com/annual-awards/winners), current [Sites of the Day](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/sites_of_the_day/), and awarded personal portfolios. Inspect live compositions and representative navigation/scrolling, then compare the ideas with Razim's actual content. Award status, award date and live-site freshness are separate facts.

This study adds six references to the two from the initial pass. Across both passes, seven live portfolio/studio sites were inspected; one additional portfolio was examined through an Awwwards recording because its linked domain is now parked. Razim's current opening was also inspected at desktop size. Submitted mobile compositions were examined for Maria and Gianluca. These are targeted observations, not accessibility, physical-device or performance audits of the references.

## What the stronger references have in common

1. **A strong first composition.** An unmistakable focal image or typographic hierarchy. The useful lesson is scale and authorship rather than a particular effect.
2. **Motion follows a visual language.** Rules, masks, curves, annotations or imagery recur coherently. Unrelated registry animations would weaken that connection.
3. **Density changes across the page.** An expressive opening gives way to clear lists, then a distinctive close. Sections do not all need equal visual intensity.
4. **Text lists can be visually rich.** Services/projects gain character from type, alignment, spacing and state feedback without becoming large illustrated panels.
5. **Real material does much of the work.** Photos, projects and individual stories support the gallery effects. Razim's strongest available material is his portrait, service scope and professional background.

These are judgments about fit, not measured conversion/performance scores.

## Revised direction

Choose the personal editorial studio foundation. Keep green/Geist and the actual portrait, but make portrait and type share one more deliberate composition. Remove floating miniature interfaces from the opening; replace their visual weight with an authored green frame/rule treatment.

Services become a compact index with explicit outcome, scope disclosure and enquiry action. On desktop, category/introduction and service rows can share a two-column arrangement. On phones, stack them; titles/outcomes remain visible without hover. Category and selection transitions connect actual choices to the planner summary.

The dominant motion moment belongs to the opening: one coordinated portrait/type transition with a clear finished state. The same frame/rule language supports category changes and a restrained section boundary. Give the footer a distinct composition and local feedback. Career content stays readable immediately.

**Change from the initial plan:** a new large morphing scope-sheet scene is no longer a default requirement. First prototype existing delivery content with a simple stage/progress transition. Add an elaborate scene only if a preview shows that it explains the work clearly and earns the space. Concentrate expressive motion around the actual person and actual interaction state.

## Implementation consequences

The selected direction needs typography, layout, CSS and state/layout motion. Keep Next/React, Tailwind, Motion and existing primitives. Award-site technology tags describe their submissions; they do not establish requirements for this site. The chosen composition does not require another animation, scrolling or 3D library.

Use official APIs against installed versions and measure the implementation. No frame-rate, loading, bundle-size or conversion claim was established by this research. Reduced motion, immediate keyboard feedback, touch access and retained drafts remain requirements.

No third-party code, fonts, assets or videos were adopted or downloaded. Exact reusable components selected later still need source-revision and license verification through the existing provenance workflow.
