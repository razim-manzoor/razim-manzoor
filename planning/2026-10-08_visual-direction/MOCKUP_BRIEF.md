# Visual-study brief

Authorized 2026-10-08: create the two concrete mockups described in UI_PLAN.md. Neither is the selected production direction. This development brief is outside the served mockup folder.

Mode: Persuade for the portfolio; Operate inside the optional planner. Audience, facts and existing behavioral boundaries come from PRODUCT.md. Source content/assets come from the earlier standalone preview. No new claims, completed project evidence or application changes.

## Direction A: Portrait and illustrated panels

THESIS: show the real person and the kinds of useful work together; the visual improvement must be visible at rest.

OWN-WORLD: existing Geist/green identity, authentic rectangular photograph in a deep-green architectural frame, crisp interface diagrams on pale-green fields, open reading sections.

STORY: recognise Razim, understand the offered systems from example scenes, explore the existing needs and start an optional conversation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: name and actions on the left; real portrait dominant in the right composition, two smaller explanatory fragments. The first illustrated service region begins immediately after the audience selector.

FORM: the explicitly specified portrait/panels study from the user-approved plan. An extension of the existing visual identity; no new identity or randomized concept round. Compare with direction B rather than treating A as approved.

FINISH: this study ends with fresh desktop/mobile/theme evidence, a finish review and an accurate mockup record. Production integration is a later task after direction selection.

## Direction B: Workflow and open rows

THESIS: make the transition from business request to usable interface the dominant authored moment.

OWN-WORLD: the same identity and colors; a smaller authentic portrait beside the name and a large flat geometric workflow composition. Service descriptions join open rows rather than repeated identical boxes.

STORY: meet the person, see what a connected system does, then understand individual service options and the delivery path.

FIRST VIEWPORT: personal identity and contact on the left, a clearly captioned example workflow on the right; portrait supports identity rather than owning the main illustration field.

FORM: the explicitly specified workflow/rows study from the user-approved plan. Same facts and controls as A; meaningful structural comparison.

FINISH: same evidence/review boundary as A. Both studies remain proposals and use semantic text and real photograph, not generated screenshots standing in for content.

## Quality bar and review scope

- Richer hero, service and delivery composition in static screenshots.
- Real photograph unchanged; supplied copy/facts retained.
- Five coordinated countable SVG/HTML scenes, not shaded pictures approximated by SVG.
- A and B distinguishable without reading the review toolbar.
- Rendered documents at 1440px and 390px in light/dark; 320px overflow check. Browser viewport override proved ineffective, so evidence uses responsive iframe layout widths; this is not native-viewport or physical-phone proof.
- Finite explanatory motion, static reduced-motion presentation and working review controls.
- Standalone preview behavior is representative, not proof of the real application's estimator, routes or draft implementation.
